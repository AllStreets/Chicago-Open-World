**Do not use the DEM's water surfaces.** The DEM is hydro-flattened: the river and the nearshore lake both read 576.1 and offshore reads 574.8. The real lake was about 580.8 that month, so these values are artefacts. Water levels come from regulation and gauge data instead.

## Corrections to the plan

1. **RIVER_Y −6.0 → −6.3.** The model flattens Upper Wacker to y 0. Measured from Upper Wacker, the river at normal pool is **6.83 m** down, so −6.0 is 0.83 m short and outside D0-2's ±0.7 m tolerance. −6.3 is the deepest value that keeps the plan's lake-step invariant (≤ 1.7 m). It equals the river at the top of its regulated band (6.34 m).
2. **LAKE_Y −4.6 → −4.65** (measured 4.28 m below the lakefront grade). This keeps the step at 1.65 m, avoiding a floating-point edge at exactly 1.7.
3. **The lock step is mostly grade difference, not head.** The real head is 0.6 m. The 1.65 m model step exists because the Loop stands about 2 m higher than the lakefront grade (the Upper Wacker frame versus the lakefront frame). D5-1's "hide it in the lock chamber" is still the right treatment.
4. **"CCD = 579.88 ft"** is the old mean-tide (NGVD-era) figure. In NAVD88 it is 579.3.
5. **RIVERWALK_Y −5.0 → −5.3.** Measured 5.77 m below Upper Wacker. The model keeps the real 1.0 m above the water.
6. **LOWER_Y −5.0 → −5.1 and SLAB_M 0.8 → 0.9.** LiDAR confirms about 5.0 m, and the structure (13′9″ clearance plus 0.94 m deck) gives about 5.2 m. The model values give 4.2 m clear.
7. **LOWER2_Y −9.5 is kept.** Measured level spacing is 4.0–4.4 m. In reality this level sits about 2.9 m *above* the river; in the model it sits below RIVER_Y, behind the dockwall, as the plan intended.
8. **D0-3 lines.** The Blue Line does not cross at "Milwaukee-Kinzie". It crosses the **South Branch twice: at Lake St and at Congress**. Both crossings were hand-mined and are deeper than the Red Line. The Red Line crosses the Main Branch at State. "Blue/Dearborn" does not cross the river; it reaches Lower Wacker only via the Lake St tube.
9. **The 322 `layer<0` ways** is the core-cache count. The world cache has **529**. Of those, 312 are inside the downtown multi-level zone (`LOWER_ZONE`). Most of the rest are rail underpasses and expressway trenches on the South and West Sides, and they must not become decks.
10. **Tube depth.** The plan says "−16 m". The real Red Line rail is about 15 m below the water, which puts it at **≈ −21 m** in the model; Blue is deeper, about −22 m, marked approximate. Both −16 and −21 satisfy the ceiling rule.

## What D1 must know

- Read every constant from `levels.json`. It holds RIVER_Y −6.3, RIVERWALK_Y −5.3, LOWER_Y −5.1, SLAB_M 0.9, LAKE_Y −4.65 and LOWER2_Y −9.5.
- **Tube ramps.** Lake/State station is only about 170 m from the river edge. Going from SUBWAY_Y −9 down to −21 at the 4 % RAMP_SLOPE needs about 300 m, which does not fit. The real tube does this at about 3.5 % from a deeper station: real rail depth is 12–13 m below the street, and the model uses −9. Three options:
  - accept the plan's −16 at the river, which needs about 4 %;
  - steepen the river dips to about 6 %;
  - lower the stations near the river.
- **Bridge decks** really sit 0.5–4 m above Upper Wacker (approach median 606.7; the DuSable upper deck is 613.8). Deck-to-water is therefore 7–11 m, not 6.3. D1-4 pits and piers should use RIVER_Y. The DuSable lower deck is at the Upper Wacker level and the upper deck is 4.3 m above it (relevant to D2-4).
- **Lower Wacker column bays are 32 ft (9.75 m).** D2-2 currently says "every 9 m".
- **Beaches and revetments (D5).** Real edge tops are about 2.6 m above the lake. Step the aprons from y 0 down to about LAKE_Y + 2.6, then down to the water.
- **D2-1** should use `lowerLevelZone` (312 ways). The compact JSON measures 36.8 KB, against a 150 KB budget.
